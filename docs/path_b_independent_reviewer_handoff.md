# Path B independent reviewer handoff — recruitment and separation

**Status:** practical coordination instructions, not an independent review and not
a protocol freeze. PI approved Path B as the preferred *development* route on
2026-10-11. This file is intended for the PI/coordinator, **not** as source
material for a blinded reviewer.

## Immediate human role: one independent HL7 v2 laboratory-interface specialist

Minimum expertise: hands-on ORU^R01 laboratory interface implementation or
conformance validation, and ability to interpret HL7 v2.5.1/LRI normative
usage, conditions, datatypes and cross-field rules. IGAMT/NIST experience is a
plus rather than an absolute requirement.

Ask for a scoped commitment to independently enumerate the relevant
Common + NG + FRU Edition 5/2024DEC requirements for the bounded ORU^R01
result slice, covering MSH, PID, ORC, OBR, OBX, SPM, and NTE if used.
Require page/table citations, exact datatype and field constraints, bindings,
conditional predicates, unresolved ambiguities, and the declared independence
metadata. The task is a *standards review*, not an implementation of our
existing conversion software.

**Blindness:** the reviewer must receive the official, verified-hash guide
and errata, plus the neutral ZIP generated by
`qualification/hl7v2/required_element_review/build_blind_packet.py`.
Do not supply Renderer A code, provisional inventory, our tests, AI reader
outputs, contract proposals, or guessed rules before the initial response is
locked. Keep the raw first-round response and role disclosure immutable.

A clean-context, separately disclosed AI-assisted extraction may serve as
reader A for development; the independent human reads the normative source
without seeing it. It is **not** legitimate to report the AI reader as a human
expert. A separate qualified human must adjudicate all disagreements and
audit a prospectively selected sample of agreements **and source completeness**.

## Second human role: independent standards adjudicator

Can be another HL7/lab-interface expert, ideally with no access to the
readers' first-stage responses until both are locked. Examine the full union
of reported rules, all disagreements, a preselected random audit of
agreements, **plus potential omissions missed by both readers**. Missing or
unresolved R/C constraints cannot be silently treated as passes.

## Later roles: two clinical laboratory annotators

Clinical laboratory scientists/medical laboratory professionals experienced
in LIS reports, specimen/order identities, corrected results, and units.
Both independently review prespecified pilot cases blinded to the verifier.
They first record free-text clinically meaningful source/recipient
differences; categories and severity are mapped later. Do not assign the
second label *only* to cases where the verifier disagrees.

A qualified adjudicator resolves disagreement without seeing verifier
predictions. Statistical input and PI approval remain necessary before
the later sealed confirmatory study.

## What the PI must actually arrange

1. Identify one independent HL7 v2 laboratory-interface specialist and
   ascertain a realistic time commitment, conflict history, and whether
   participation is paid or an in-kind academic collaboration.
2. Identify a separate standards adjudicator (later), and two qualified
   laboratory annotators (before the expert pilot), with conflicts checked.
3. Check institutional policies on ethics determinations and specialist
   compensation before expert annotation begins.
4. Later approve the actual prospective estimand and sample-size plan with
   statistical input. No new GitHub configuration or standards emails are
   prerequisites to continued software development.

**Not yet resolved:** concrete reviewer identities, effort, honoraria,
independent completeness denominator, error-policy adjudication, validator
qualification, clinical labels, sample size, or external ethics determination.

## Exit condition for the next milestone

Completed blind extraction responses; source-hash and independent-identity
checks; a documented adjudication and completeness audit; an explicitly
bounded R/C coverage denominator; and evidence that no unreviewed mandatory
requirement is being called valid. Only then may a provisional G1 rule
implementation enter qualification. No confirmatory model inference or
sealed-case opening before a separate frozen protocol approval.
